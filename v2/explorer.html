    About this data

    Products are found live in NASA's archive at the Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF DAAC). The images on the globe are the official quick-look PNGs: 8-bit, stretched per image, so brightness is relative within one image. Comparisons between dates are a guide, not a measurement.

    The full products (HDF5, often 2 GB) carry calibrated values and need a free NASA Earthdata login to download. NISAR products are still provisional.

    Cycle numbers come from the product names. NISAR repeats its ground track every 12 days (173 orbits), so each track and frame is imaged once per cycle.
